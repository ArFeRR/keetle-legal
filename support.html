Keetle · kettle boil-timer for iPhone

Keetle times how long your kettle takes to boil — by physics, not guesswork — and shows a live countdown on your Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, widgets and Control Center.

Getting started

  • Add your kettle — pick it from the catalog of 2,600+ models, scan its barcode or label, or enter wattage and capacity by hand.
  • Tap Boil — Keetle estimates the time from your kettle's power, fill level, and starting water temperature.
  • Walk away — follow the countdown in the app, on the Lock Screen, or in the Dynamic Island; a notification tells you when the water is ready.

Common questions

The time isn't exact. Keetle shows an approximate time; real kettles vary with scale, voltage, and lid. A one-time calibration boil sharpens future estimates.

Room temperature looks off. Allow location for automatic local weather, or tap the temperature to set it yourself.

I have a stovetop kettle. Add it as a stovetop kettle — you choose the stove and burner when you boil.

No notification when the water is ready. Check iOS Settings → Keetle → Notifications, and whether a Focus mode is silencing it.

The countdown isn't on my Lock Screen. Make sure Live Activities are on in iOS Settings → Keetle.

Premium. Keetle Premium is a subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, billed by Apple. Manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. A free trial converts automatically unless cancelled before it ends.

Restoring purchases. Use “Restore purchases” in Keetle's Settings or on the paywall to recover Premium on a new device.
